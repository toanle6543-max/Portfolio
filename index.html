Team Structural Design Project · Dec 2025 – Jun 2026 · San Diego, CA

PCI Big Beam Competition

National Precast/Prestressed Concrete Design Competition · Mar – Jun 2026

A 4-person team designed, fabricated, and load-tested a precast, pretensioned concrete I-beam against PCI's national rule set — a 17-ft simply supported span carrying 32 kips of total factored load without cracking under service load.

I derived sectional properties and prestress losses to guide the initial design, then modeled deflections across unfactored, factored, cracking, and peak load stages to select rebar eccentricity and allowable stresses that avoided top-fiber tension at transfer — while also tracking cost and weight, since PCI scores both directly.

C.L.A.S.P.

Classical Lamination Analytical Solver Program · Independent Software Project · Dec 2025 – Jul 2026

A browser-based Classical Laminate Theory solver — rebuilt from an original MATLAB tool into a single-file, dependency-free web app that runs entirely offline. It computes the full ABD stiffness matrix, ply-by-ply stress and strain recovery, and first-ply failure loads under Max Stress, Max Strain, and Hashin–Rotem criteria, with an iterative bisection search for independent per-component load scaling.

Because a solver is only as good as its numbers, I re-derived the ABD matrix independently in a separate line-by-line implementation and cross-checked it against the original MATLAB output — matching to 6 significant figures across multiple layups, including thermal loading cases — then backed it with a Playwright regression suite covering both UI behavior and numerical correctness.
